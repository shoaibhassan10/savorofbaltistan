Section 01

Visual Layout Guidelines

Authority & Provenance Placement

In an industry saturated with counterfeit Shilajit and adulterated herbal products, consumer distrust is the primary purchase barrier. Placing the Savor of Baltistan Logo and the Founder’s Mountain Portrait strategically transforms the brand from an anonymous supplier into a personal, high-integrity guarantee.

The Official Brand Emblem

Three aerodynamic green shoots arching dynamically upward to symbolize alpine vitality, pure mountain flora, and upward growth. Anchored by the bold wordmark SAVOR OF BALTISTAN.

  • Clear Space: Maintain minimum 32px padding around the circular emblem.
  • Placement: Fixed top-left in web navigation; centered on product jar lids and sealed kraft pouches.
  • Orientation: The green shoots must always arch towards the right (symbolizing ascension and progress).
  • Backgrounds: Crisp white, obsidian dark slate (#0a1118), or raw unbleached kraft paper.

The Founder Trust Anchor

The authentic photograph of the founder wearing the traditional woollen Balti cap (pakol) and clean white attire, framed by an alpine glacial lake and the towering Karakoram range.

  • Placement: Prominently featured on the "About Us" page, Homepage Trust strip, and unboxing card.
  • Emotional Function: Visually proves indigenous connection to Ghanche; removes corporate anonymity.
  • Typography Pairing: Always accompany with a signed personal quote: "Born in Ghanche. Handcrafted with ancestral integrity."
  • Treatment: Natural mountain sunlight. Never apply heavy AI beauty filters or artificial studio backdrops.

From the Granite Heights of Ghanche to Islamabad: The Story of Shoaib Hassan

My name is Shoaib Hassan. I was born in Ghanche, a high-altitude district in Gilgit-Baltistan cradled beneath the massive peaks of the Karakoram. In our valleys, nature does not offer easy shortcuts. Life is shaped by crisp glacial air, thin atmosphere, and rocky soil that demands patient respect. What the mountains do yield, however, is of unrivaled concentration: medicinal herbs that survive freezing scree slopes, apricots cured golden under raw alpine sunshine, and the sacred black mineral resin that seeps from ancient rock faces every summer—Shilajit.

After completing my primary schooling in Skardu, I set out for Islamabad to pursue higher education, currently completing my degree in Computer Science (explore my technical portfolio at shoaibhassan10.me and personal journey on Instagram @shoaibhassan_10). I carried with me the hope of being the first in my family to build an enterprise of international caliber—one that would make my parents, my elders, and my homeland proud.

"In Islamabad’s bustling markets, I witnessed sellers marketing diluted, mass-produced molasses as 'Pure Himalayan Shilajit'. I knew the truth. I knew what pure Shilajit smelled like, tasted like, and how it was collected by families in Ghanche."

The world was paying premium prices for diluted counterfeits, while the indigenous harvesters of Ghanche—who preserve centuries of authentic botanical knowledge—remained cut off from the global audience.

Savor of Baltistan was founded to eliminate every intermediary. By blending my computer science training with my indigenous heritage, I established a direct pipeline connecting Ghanche's mountains directly to your home. When you order from Savor of Baltistan, you are not buying from a broker. You are receiving products verified by someone who grew up on the land.

Section 05

Sensory Product Portfolio

Descriptions & Medicinal Profiles
Flagship Mineral Resin · Single-Origin Ghanche

Raw Karakoram Shilajit

Drawn from high-altitude rock fissures above 3,500 meters in Ghanche, our Shilajit is harvested during the brief summer thaw when ancient rock seams warm under intense ultraviolet light. It features an earthy, petrified botanical aroma, a deep obsidian resinous gloss, and a pungent, distinctly bitter mineral profile. Rich in bioactive fulvic acid and 84+ trace minerals, it promotes cellular ATP production, stamina, and mental acuity.

Ritual: Dissolve a pea-sized resin portion (300–500mg) in warm water or raw milk every morning.
Wild Karakoram Apothecary · Ghanche Mountain Herbs

Traditional Healing Herbs: Shamdun, Teri & Makhoting

Wild-harvested along glacial streams and rugged scree slopes in Ghanche, these three herbs form the cornerstone of traditional Balti wellness. Naturally dried in clean mountain air to preserve their delicate essential oils and bio-compounds.

🌿 Shamdun

Blood pressure regulation, cellular hydration, fat loss & active lipid metabolism.

🍃 Teri

Natural kidney stone flushing, liver detoxification & cooling of internal inflammation.

🌱 Makhoting

Rapid relief from acute stomach aches, cramps, bloating & intestinal ecosystem balance.

Heritage Orchard · Mountain Sun-Dried

Premium Dry Fruits & Nuts Collection

Sun-cured in the pristine mountain atmosphere without sulphites or synthetic preserving agents. Includes small, honey-sweet Baltistani Apricots (Khubani), thin-shelled buttery Karakoram Walnuts (Akhrot), nutrient-dense Wild Almonds (Badam), high-altitude Pistachios (Pista), golden sun-ripened Figs (Anjeer), and prized sweet Apricot Kernels (Geeri).

Artisanal Elixirs · Cold-Pressed & Raw

Cold-Pressed Apricot Kernel Oil & Wild Honey

Silky, golden apricot kernel oil gently cold-pressed on traditional wooden expellers—extraordinary as a nutrient-dense restorative facial serum and hair tonic. Paired with unheated, monofloral wild Karakoram honey collected from high-altitude bee colonies foraging on wild mountain thyme, mint, and clover.
