Quick Answers

Common Questions

How do I place an order?

The easiest way is via WhatsApp or through our Facebook/Instagram DMs. We're also happy to take orders via email. We don't yet have a payment gateway, but payment instructions are provided with each order.

Do you ship outside Pakistan?

Yes, for international orders please contact us directly. We'll provide you with shipping costs and timelines based on your location. Dry fruits and honey travel particularly well.

How do I know the Shilajit is genuine?

Authentic Shilajit has a strong mineral smell, bitter taste, and dissolves completely in warm water. Our founder is from the exact region of harvest — his origin is the most credible proof of source. You're welcome to ask him anything directly.

Can I order gift sets for Eid or weddings?

Absolutely. We offer beautifully curated gift sets with custom options. Contact us with your requirements and event date and we'll arrange the rest.
