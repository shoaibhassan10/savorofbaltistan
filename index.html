From the Rock to Your Door

The Shilajit Journey

No chain is too long for purity. Ours has four steps and zero compromises.

01

Summer Thaw

When Karakoram peaks warm each summer, Shilajit seeps from ancient rock faces above 3,000m — harvested by hand in a small seasonal window.

02

Verified at Source

Our founder — born in Ghanche — personally knows the harvesters and the terrain. No blind purchasing. No middlemen. Direct accountability.

03

Minimal Processing

The resin is cleaned of surface rock and debris — nothing more. No heat, no blending, no fillers. What the mountain gives is what you receive.

04

Delivered to You

Packaged carefully and dispatched with full traceability. A product with a story, a source, and a face behind it — ready for your daily ritual.

The Founder's Story
"I grew up watching the mountains give everything they had. I started this business so the world could finally receive it."

Born in Ghanche, raised in the shadow of the Karakoram — the founder of Savor of Baltistan grew up with Shilajit, apricots, and mountain honey as ordinary parts of life. Now studying Computer Science in Islamabad, he is building a direct bridge from his homeland to the world.
