Authenticity Note: Real Shilajit has a strong, distinct mineral smell and a bitter, earthy taste. It dissolves completely in warm water or milk. If your Shilajit is odourless, tasteless, or powdery — it is not authentic.

Shilajit has been used in Ayurvedic and traditional Balti medicine for centuries. Modern research continues to explore its composition, which includes fulvic acid, humic acid, and over 80 trace minerals. Our Shilajit — raw, unprocessed, and single-origin — provides the purest possible starting point for these traditional uses.

  • Rich in fulvic acid — a natural compound that supports mineral absorption
  • Over 80 naturally occurring trace minerals from the Karakoram geological environment
  • Traditionally used to support energy, stamina, and cognitive clarity
  • Adaptogenic properties — used for millennia in high-altitude communities
  • Antioxidant properties supported by its humic acid content

These statements reflect traditional use and ongoing research. Savor of Baltistan does not make medical claims. Consult a healthcare provider before use.

Daily Ritual: Dissolve a pea-sized portion (approximately 300–500mg) in warm water, milk, or green tea. Stir until fully dissolved — authentic resin will dissolve completely. Consume once daily, preferably in the morning.

  • Start with a smaller amount (rice grain size) and increase gradually
  • Best consumed on an empty stomach or with warm milk
  • Avoid combining with chlorinated tap water — use filtered or warm milk
  • Consistent daily use over 4–8 weeks is recommended for noticeable effect
  • Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight

The Dissolve Test: Place a small amount in warm water. Authentic Shilajit will dissolve fully, turning the water a rich golden-brown. Any residue or floating particles indicate impurity.

How do I know it's authentic?
Authentic Shilajit has a strong mineral smell, a bitter-earthy taste, and dissolves completely in warm liquid. Our product meets all these criteria. The founder is personally reachable for any question.

Is yours different from Indian or Nepali Shilajit brands?
Yes. Our Shilajit is single-origin from a specific district in Pakistan (Ghanche), at verified high altitude, with no blending. Most global brands aggregate from multiple sources. Ours does not.

How long does one jar last?
At the recommended dose of 300–500mg per day, a 20g jar lasts approximately 40–65 days.

Can I take it with medication?
Consult your healthcare provider before combining Shilajit with prescription medications, particularly blood thinners or hypoglycaemic agents.
